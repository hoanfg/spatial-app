# Measurement sensitivity validation: Yanqing, Beijing (2020, 500 m)

**Date:** 11 November 2026. **Sample:** 8,333 Yanqing (Diên Khánh) grid cells, `CHN.2.1.11_1`, year 2020. **Application reference:** commit `9574cf872c52d266aa15c363df7965cce31601f0` after PR #10.

## Main conclusions

**Data consistency and measurement sensitivity were validated within this pilot:**
- The masked median and zero-filled median are **exactly identical** for all 30,814 pixels of the downloaded raster; masked median has **zero NoData pixels**. Retaining the mask therefore does not change NTL/Gini/Moran/LISA for this sample.
- Switching from annual median to annual mean increases the unweighted NTL average by **4.366%**, from **1.4137188** to **1.4754449 nW/cm²/sr**. NTL Gini increases from **0.6809244** to **0.6861015**.
- Binary-Queen Global Moran I across all cells rises from **0.914128499** to **0.914558970**. This is an independently scripted all-sample Moran result and need not equal a different quantity in the LISA panel.
- Under the same LISA procedure (999 conditional permutations **without replacement**, seed 42, two-sided tails, tie tolerance `1e−12`, BH-FDR alpha 0.05), HH increases from **370** to **385** and LL decreases from **2,510** to **2,499**. HH Jaccard is **0.96104**, LL Jaccard **0.96201**.
- The median baseline reproduces **100% of LISA labels** in the app CSV, isolating the monthly-image aggregation effect from simulation discrepancies.

## Limitations to disclose in publication

1. There are no missing pixels in Yanqing 2020, so **this pilot cannot quantify bias from `unmask(0)` under genuinely missing values**. Testing that assumption requires another region/year with missing data or a controlled masking experiment (which would measure only synthetic sensitivity).
2. Annual mean here is the **unweighted mean of 12 monthly composites**, not an annual mean weighted by `cf_cvg` observation counts. Neither annual mean nor median is universally the better measure.
3. LISA p-values depend on permutations, seed and BH. Using the same draws here isolates the monthly-image aggregation change; it does not establish the economic meaning of hotspots.
4. Evidence covers **one area, one year and the same VIIRS product**; cross-time stability and other-region generalization remain unverified.
5. No GHSL correlation was recomputed because GHSL per-cell data were unavailable; do not infer GDP, house prices or vacancy.
6. This analysis requires no increased Streamlit RAM and **does not modify the repository**.
