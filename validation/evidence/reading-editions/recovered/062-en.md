**Date:** 11 November 2026

## Conclusion

**PASS for all 8,333/8,333 cells:** independently computed VIIRS image means weighted by polygon-pixel overlap area match the web app's `ntl_mean` when rounded to four decimals. All absolute differences are ≤ **0.00005 nW/cm²/sr**; **no boundary exception remains**. This establishes independent reproducibility of raster aggregation onto the 500 m grid in Yanqing 2020 within exported CSV precision.

## Method

1. Use the Yanqing GeoJSON at the audited commit, restoring complete source-coordinate precision. The GeoJSON, source values and SHA-256 of input files are in the evidence bundle. GID `CHN.2.1.11_1` is unchanged.
2. Project the boundary to UTM EPSG:32650; create 500 × 500 m squares starting at coordinate multiples of 500 with a one-cell extension, then clip each cell to the boundary. This yields **exactly 8,333 polygons** in the same `grid_id` order, with area and centroid values matching their CSV-rounded values.
3. Project each **clipped polygon** to raster EPSG:4326. For each raster pixel, calculate intersection area with the polygon, multiply by `avg_rad`, and divide by total overlap area. The independent script does not import `ntl.zonal` or `exactextract`.
4. Compare independent references to CSV `ntl_mean` for each `grid_id`: all 630 clipped cells (600 edge and 30 slightly clipped), not only full squares. `yanqing_viirs_all_8333_polygon_comparison.csv` contains per-cell comparisons and errors.

## Why did an earlier comparison have one discrepant cell?

Using a boundary quantized to seven decimal places produced an error of about **0.0000643** for `grid_id 349`. Reconstructing with full source-coordinate precision reduced that error to within CSV rounding. This illustrates how **clipped cells** depend on geometric precision, rather than demonstrating a fault in the app's NTL extraction.

## Remaining scientific limitations

- This validates **reproduction of NTL under the same source and processing conventions**, not that NTL always measures GDP, economic activity or housing vacancy correctly.
- The reference raster was exported via `Export.image.toDrive`, not the exact `getDownloadURL` raster bytes used by the app. The methods can differ in grid alignment; for this dataset zonal means agree within rounding.
- Annual median, `cf_cvg > 0`, `unmask(0)` and negative clipping are processing choices requiring further sensitivity assessment before concluding measurement bias. Twelve of twelve valid months in this ROI do not imply the same in other years/locations.
- No complete Streamlit Cloud RAM benchmark, no other regions or years, and no assessment of VIIRS product accuracy against ground observations.
- With four-decimal CSV storage, the comparison cannot prove equality at full underlying unrounded numerical precision.
