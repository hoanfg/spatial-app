# Temporal validation: Yanqing, Beijing, 2014 to 2020

**Status:** PASS for reproducing Space-Time computations from exported CSV, with one rounding-sensitive threshold disagreement; **NOT YET PASS** for independent validation of the 2014 VIIRS raster, which was unavailable at the time of the report.

**Source:** `CHN_beijing_yanqing_2014_2020_500m_spacetime_cells.csv` (8,333 cells); formulas reviewed against `ntl/lisa_core.py` in `hoanfg/spatial-app`, version following PR #10 (merge SHA `9574cf872c52d266aa15c363df7965cce31601f0`). The formulas were reimplemented in separate Python code (`validate_temporal.py`), not executed through the app's functions. Validating CSV calculations is not the same as confirming the underlying 2014 satellite imagery.

## 1. Reproduced calculations

- **8,333 cells**, including **7,733 core cells** and **600 excluded edge cells**.
- `ntl_2020` equals `ntl_mean` for all 8,333 cells.
- `delta_ntl`, `delta_ln_ntl`, `z_2014`, `z_2020`, `delta_z`, `delta_lag_z` and `movement_magnitude` were reproduced from `ntl_2014`, `ntl_2020`, locations and Queen neighbors within the expected error due to NTL inputs rounded to four decimals.
- Queen neighbor counts match **8,333/8,333** exported values.
- Space-Time labels computed from the app's **exported rounded direction and movement values** match **8,333/8,333**. Recomputing directly from NTL rounded to four decimals matches **8,332/8,333** labels. For `grid_id=3922`, recomputed `movement_magnitude` is about **0.049964**, while exported CSV has **0.0500** (the threshold is 0.05), leading to one label discrepancy. This does not establish an app calculation error: CSV inputs are rounded, whereas the app uses unrounded values before export.

## 2. Actual definition of Synchronized Decline

The application uses:

`v_t = ln(1 + max(NTL_t, 0))`; `z_t = (v_t - mean(v_t on core))/(sd(v_t on core, ddof=1))`.

`delta_z = z_2020 - z_2014`; `delta_lag_z = QueenLag(z_2020) - QueenLag(z_2014)`.

Movement magnitude is `m = sqrt(delta_z² + delta_lag_z²)`. Only core cells with `m >= 0.05` belong to one of four directional regimes:

- **Synergistic Growth:** `delta_z >= 0`, `delta_lag_z >= 0`.
- **Agglomeration Shadow:** `delta_z < 0`, `delta_lag_z >= 0`.
- **Emergent Pioneer:** `delta_z >= 0`, `delta_lag_z < 0`.
- **Synchronized Decline:** `delta_z < 0`, `delta_lag_z < 0`.

**Important:** `Synchronized Decline` means **declining relative standing** (the cell and neighboring log NTL z-scores fall after year-specific standardization), **not** declining absolute NTL. These labels are not results of permutation tests or FDR; they are descriptive classifications based on direction and a movement threshold.

## 3. Results by the original web application labels

| Regime | Cells | Absolute NTL increases | Absolute NTL decreases | Average NTL 2014 | Average NTL 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Synergistic Growth | 1,456 | 1,456 | 0 | 0.481 | 3.859 |
| Agglomeration Shadow | 75 | 75 | 0 | 0.819 | 2.148 |
| Emergent Pioneer | 194 | 194 | 0 | 0.324 | 1.075 |
| **Synchronized Decline** | **4,711** | **4,624** | **87** | **0.532** | **1.029** |
| Excluded or Static | 1,897 | 1,872 | 25 | 0.201 | 0.498 |

- **98.15%** of cells classified as `Synchronized Decline` actually **increased in absolute NTL**. That group's mean almost doubled.
- `Synchronized Decline` comprises **60.92% of the 7,733 core cells** (or 56.53% of all 8,333). Most of this regime denotes decline relative to the standardized distribution, not decline in urban/economic activity.
- Across the entire region, unweighted mean NTL increased from **0.4452 to 1.4137**; **8,221 cells increased** and **112 decreased** in absolute terms, including edge cells.
- With only two endpoints in 2014 and 2020, this is a **two-point comparison**; it does not establish a continuous upward trend in the intervening years.

## 4. Spatial Markov derived from CSV

NTL quartile transition counts (Q1-Q4), measured in **cells** (sample of 7,733 core cells):

| 2014 to 2020 | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Q1 | 1,482 | 422 | 23 | 6 |
| Q2 | 446 | 1,190 | 245 | 53 |
| Q3 | 4 | 321 | 1,426 | 181 |
| Q4 | 0 | 1 | 239 | 1,694 |

- **74.90%** of cells remained in the same quartile between endpoints even as absolute regional NTL rose substantially.
- The app-source Shorrocks index is approximately **0.3347** (based on transition probabilities rounded to one percentage point decimal). It measures rank mobility, not absolute radiance growth.

## 5. Sensitivity to the movement magnitude threshold

Changing the classification threshold while holding the app's exported deltas and data fixed:

| Threshold | Synergistic Growth | Agglomeration Shadow | Emergent Pioneer | Synchronized Decline | Excluded/Static |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.025 | 1,555 | 137 | 438 | 5,242 | 961 |
| **0.050 (baseline)** | **1,456** | **75** | **194** | **4,711** | **1,897** |
| 0.100 | 1,254 | 42 | 62 | 2,949 | 4,026 |
| 0.200 | 998 | 23 | 20 | 1,111 | 6,181 |

Thus, **counts assigned to movement regimes are quite threshold-sensitive**. This does not prove the algorithm is wrong, but the results should be treated as descriptive analysis with assumptions to report. Near the threshold, rounding on export can cause some cells to switch group when recalculated from CSV.

## 6. Conclusions and application recommendations

1. **PASS: formulas and CSV classifications.** Independent checks are consistent; `grid_id=3922` is a CSV rounding-sensitive case near 0.05.
2. **UI interpretation/labels need clarification:** rather than interpreting `Synchronized Decline` as a reduction in light or economy, use "Relative Z-score decline of cell and spatial neighborhood." Likewise, `Synergistic Growth` does not by itself establish high economic growth.
3. **Space-Time labels do not imply statistical significance:** these computations do not use permutations or FDR; do not call regimes statistically significant without an additional test.
4. The `movement_magnitude=0.05` threshold should be disclosed and robustness across several thresholds reported.
5. **2014 input not validated in this report:** obtain a VIIRS 2014 raster from an independent Earth Engine export to compare all 8,333 cells as for 2020. For temporal consistency, consider intermediate years 2015-2019, not only endpoints.
6. Claims about **GDP, urbanization or economic effects** still need external validation; NTL change does not automatically establish GDP change.
